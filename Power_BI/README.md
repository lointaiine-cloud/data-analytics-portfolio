# Power BI Analytics Projects

This section showcases interactive Power BI dashboards developed to demonstrate data analysis, data modeling, DAX calculations, and data visualization skills across different analytical domains.

The projects cover public sector financial analysis and environmental data analytics, demonstrating the application of business intelligence techniques to real-world datasets.

## Projects

### 1. Government Expenditure Analysis

**Overview**

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing government expenditure across European countries from 2016 to 2024.

The project explores public spending patterns, expenditure distribution across COFOG categories, and differences in government expenditure between countries.

**Key Analysis Areas**
- Government expenditure as a percentage of GDP.
- Comparison of public spending across European countries.
- Expenditure distribution by COFOG categories.
- Historical expenditure trends.
- Identification and ranking of countries by expenditure levels.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modeling

**Project Folder:** `Government Expenditure Analysis`

### 2. Climate Intelligence & Environmental Risk Analytics

**Overview**

An interactive climate analytics dashboard exploring historical climate anomalies and environmental patterns across 334 European NUTS2 regions.

Using data from the Copernicus Climate Data Store, the project analyzes 10 climate indicators covering temperature anomalies, heat extremes, precipitation patterns, and drought-related conditions.

**Key Analysis Areas**
- Historical climate anomaly trends (1940–2025).
- Temperature and extreme heat analysis.
- Precipitation and drought indicators.
- Regional comparison of positive and negative climate anomalies.
- Geographic visualization of climate patterns across European NUTS2 regions.

**Dashboard Features**
- Interactive climate indicator selection.
- Historical trend analysis.
- Top 10 regional rankings.
- Dynamic KPI indicators.
- Interactive European NUTS2 choropleth map.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Copernicus Climate Data Store
- Eurostat GISCO

**Dataset:** 574,480 records across 334 NUTS2 regions.

**Project Folder:** `Climate_Intelligence_Environmental_Risk_Analytics`
