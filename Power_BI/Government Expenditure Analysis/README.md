# Government Expenditure Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing government expenditure across European countries and COFOG (Classification of the Functions of Government) categories over the period 2016–2024.

The goal was to explore public spending patterns, compare expenditure levels between countries, identify the largest spending categories, and analyze changes over time.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Key Analytical Areas

**1. Government Expenditure by Country**
- Compared average government expenditure across European countries.
- Identified countries with the highest and lowest expenditure levels.
- Ranked countries based on average expenditure as a percentage of GDP.

**2. Government Expenditure by COFOG Category**
- Analyzed the distribution of government expenditure across functional categories.
- Identified the largest areas of public spending.
- Compared the relative importance of different expenditure categories.

**3. Annual Government Expenditure**
- Examined changes in average government expenditure between 2016 and 2024.
- Identified years with higher and lower expenditure levels.

**4. Government Expenditure Trends by Category**
- Analyzed spending trends across COFOG categories.
- Identified changes in government spending priorities over time.

## Dashboard

The interactive Power BI dashboard includes four visualizations designed to support country comparisons, category analysis, and trend identification.
