# sql-data-analytics-portfolio
SQL projects demonstrating data analysis, business metrics, CTEs, JOINs, window functions and analytical SQL.

A collection of SQL projects focused on business-oriented data analysis.

The projects demonstrate practical SQL skills, including data aggregation,
JOINs, CTEs, window functions, ranking, segmentation and analytical metrics.

## Projects

### 01. Email Campaign Performance

Analysis of email campaign performance across different operating systems.

The project calculates the number of sent, opened and visited emails and
key email marketing metrics:

- Open Rate
- Click Rate
- Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)

The analysis excludes unsubscribed accounts.

**SQL skills:** JOIN, LEFT JOIN, CTE, COUNT(DISTINCT), GROUP BY,
calculated metrics, SAFE_DIVIDE.

### 02. Sales Performance

Analysis of sales and revenue performance by country and device.

**SQL skills:** JOIN, aggregation, GROUP BY, CASE WHEN and window functions.

### 03. Customer Engagement

Analysis of customer engagement with email campaigns based on user
characteristics and email interactions.

**SQL skills:** CTE, CASE WHEN, aggregation and segmentation.

### 04. Country Performance Ranking

Analysis and ranking of countries based on account activity and email
performance.

**SQL skills:** CTE, UNION ALL, window functions, PARTITION BY,
RANK and DENSE_RANK.
