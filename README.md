## 📂 Projects

### SQL

#### Email Campaign Performance
Analysis of email campaign performance, including email delivery, opens, visits and conversion-related metrics.

**Skills:** SQL, CTEs, JOINs, aggregations, CASE WHEN, date calculations

#### Sales Performance Analysis
Analysis of sales performance across products, countries and other business dimensions.

**Skills:** SQL, CTEs, JOINs, aggregations, window functions

#### Customer Engagement Analysis
Analysis of customer engagement and email activity using segmentation and calculated metrics.

**Skills:** SQL, CTEs, segmentation, window functions, aggregations

#### Country Performance Ranking
Analysis of account and email activity by country with country-level totals and ranking.

**Skills:** SQL, UNION ALL, window functions, DENSE_RANK

---

### Excel / Google Sheets

#### Retail Sales and Shipping Analysis
Analysis of retail sales performance, including salesperson performance, average order value, sales dynamics, customer types, shipping costs, delivery time and regional logistics.

**Skills:** Excel, Pivot Tables, calculations, regression analysis, business analysis

#### Sales Performance and Business Analysis
Comprehensive analysis of sales performance across product categories, countries, regions and sales channels. Includes KPI analysis, revenue and profit analysis, shipping performance, sales dynamics, weekday patterns and ABC analysis.

**Skills:** Excel, KPIs, Pivot Tables, ABC analysis, sales analysis, geographic analysis

---

### Python

#### Sales Data Quality and Business Analysis
End-to-end sales analysis in Python, starting with data quality auditing and data cleaning. The project includes data transformation, revenue and profit analysis, category and geographic analysis, sales channels, shipping performance, sales dynamics, weekday analysis, ABC analysis and an interactive dashboard.

**Skills:** Python, Pandas, data cleaning, data transformation, visualization, business analysis

#### E-commerce Sales and User Behavior Analysis
End-to-end analysis of e-commerce sales and user behavior using BigQuery and Python. The project covers data quality assessment, sales analysis, product categories, geography, traffic channels, devices, user segmentation, correlation analysis and statistical hypothesis testing.

**Skills:** BigQuery, SQL, Python, Pandas, statistics, correlation analysis, hypothesis testing

---

### Statistics

#### A/B Test Sample Size Analysis
Calculation of the required sample size for an A/B test based on baseline conversion, expected effect, significance level and statistical power.

The project also demonstrates how sample size changes when the significance level or expected effect changes.

**Skills:** A/B testing, statistical power, effect size, sample size calculation, Python, Statsmodels
